# Electric vehicles

Pew research results regarding consideration of buying electric vehicle.

## Usage

``` r
EVs_region
```

## Format

A data frame with 5085 observations on the following 2 variables.

- `region`:

  a factor with levels `Urban, Suburban` and `Rural`

- `consideration`:

  a factor with levels `Not too or not at all likely`,
  `Very or somewhat likely` and `Do not expect to buy a vehicle`

## Source

Pew Research Center, Survey of U.S. adults conducted April 28-May 4,
2025. "Americans' Views on Energy at the Start of Trump's Second Term"
