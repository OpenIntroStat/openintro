# Brazil death anxiety

The dataset consists of responses from 200 individuals from Brazil. The
main measure is the DAQ, a 15-item multidimensional scale that measures
fears related to suffering, loneliness, the unknown, and personal
extinction (see Variable Description). The scale measures range from 0
(“not at all”) to 8 (“very much”). In addition, the file contains a
free-list measure of participants’ fears and worries, which may be
coded, and several demographic variables.

## Usage

``` r
Brazil_death_anxiety
```

## Format

A data frame with 200 rows and 28 variables.

- participant:

  Participant id

- gender:

  Gender of pariticipant.

- year_born:

  Year participant was born.

- age_in_2015:

  Age of participant when data was collected.

- ethnicity:

  Ethnicity of participant.

- marital_status:

  Marital status of participant.

- employment:

  Employment status of participant.

- occupation:

  Occupation of participant.

- fivefears_1:

  The first entry in a free list of participants’ fears and worries.

- fivefears_2:

  The second entry in a free list of participants’ fears and worries.

- fivefears_3:

  The third entry in a free list of participants’ fears and worries.

- fivefears_4:

  The fourth entry in a free list of participants’ fears and worries.

- fivefears_5:

  The fifth entry in a free list of participants’ fears and worries.

- worry_death:

  Do you worry about dying?

- incomplete_bucket_list:

  Does it bother you that you may die before you have done everything
  you wanted to?

- deathly_ill:

  Do you worry that you may be very ill for a long time before you die?

- concern_for_others:

  Does it upset you to think that others may see you suffering when you
  die?

- painful_death:

  Do you worry that dying may be very painful?

- loved_ones:

  Do you worry that the persons most close to you won’t be with you when
  you are dying?

- die_alone:

  Do you worry that you may be alone when you are dying?

- cognitive_function:

  Does the thought bother you that you might lose control of your mind
  before death?

- expenses_burden:

  Do you worry that expenses connected with your dying will be a burden
  for other people?

- as_you_wish:

  Does it worry you that your instructions or will about your belongings
  may not be carried out after you die?

- buried_alive:

  Are you afraid that you may be buried before you are really dead?

- left_behind:

  Does the thought of leaving loved ones behind you when you die disturb
  you?

- remembered:

  Do you worry that those you care about may not remember you after your
  death?

- permenant:

  Does the thought worry you that with death you may be gone forever?

- after_death:

  Are you worried about not knowing what to expect after death?

## Source

Jong, J., Halberstadt, J., Bluemke, M. et al. Death anxiety, exposure to
death, mortuary preferences, and religiosity in five countries. Sci Data
