# Poll on climate change activism

A Pew Research poll published in May of 2021 looks at how Americans'
attitudes about climate change differ by generation, party and other
factors.

## Usage

``` r
generation_climate_action
```

## Format

A data frame with 13664 observations on the following 2 variables.

- `generation`:

  a factor with levels `Gen Z, Millenial, Gen X` and `Boomer & older`

- `response`:

  a factor with levels `Took action` and `Did not take action`

## Source

Pew Research Center, American Trends Panel. Survey conducted April 20 -
29, 2021. "Gen Z, Millennials Stand Out for Climate Change Activism,
Social Media Engagement With Issue"
